# MultiTimeBar Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-05_

MultiTimeBar is designed to respect your privacy completely.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** MultiTimeBar does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal
information, analytics, telemetry, crash reports, device identifiers, or usage
data of any kind.

## Network access

MultiTimeBar does not make any network requests. The app does not communicate with
any server operated by us or by any third party.

## Data storage

The clocks you add and the settings you configure are stored locally on your
Mac in the standard `UserDefaults` container for the app. This data never
leaves your device.

You can remove all MultiTimeBar data at any time by uninstalling the app and
deleting its container:

```sh
rm -rf ~/Library/Containers/com.ferin79.multitimebar
```

## Third parties

MultiTimeBar does not use any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, analytics
providers, or crash reporting services.

## Children

MultiTimeBar does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including
children under 13.

## Contact

Questions? Open an issue at the project's GitHub repository, listed in the
support URL of the App Store listing.
