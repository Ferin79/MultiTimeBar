Welcome to MultiTimeBar!

This first release brings every time zone that matters to your macOS menu bar:
- Multiple clocks side-by-side with country flags and live times
- 12 or 24-hour formats with optional seconds
- Day-difference indicators (+1d / -1d) so you never book the wrong day
- Time Travel planner to preview a meeting time across every clock at once
- Stack clocks in two rows for compact display on notched MacBooks
- City search across major cities and the IANA time zone database
- Optional Launch at Login (opt-in via Settings)
- Fully sandboxed, on-device, zero telemetry

Thanks for installing — feedback and contributions are welcome on GitHub.
