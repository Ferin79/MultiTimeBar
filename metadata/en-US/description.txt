MultiTimeBar is the fastest way to see the time in every city that matters to you — right from the macOS menu bar.

Whether you work with a distributed team, coordinate with family abroad, or just enjoy following markets in different regions, MultiTimeBar keeps every clock a glance away. Add as many time zones as you like. No paywalls, no accounts, no ads.

WHY MULTITIME
• Every feature is free, always. No "Pro" upsell.
• 100% native SwiftUI. Beautiful on every Mac, from Intel to Apple silicon.
• Zero telemetry. Your city list never leaves your device.
• Open source under the MIT license.

FEATURES
• Multiple time zones side-by-side in the menu bar
• Country flag emojis for at-a-glance identification
• 12 or 24-hour format, with optional seconds
• Day difference indicators (+1d, -1d) so you never book a meeting for the wrong day
• Stack clocks in two rows for compact display
• Time Travel planner: drag a slider to see when a meeting time lands in every zone
• City search across major cities and IANA time zones
• Reorder, rename, and remove clocks with a click
• Optional Launch at login (opt-in via Settings, powered by SMAppService)
• All settings and data stored locally in UserDefaults

PRIVACY
MultiTimeBar does not connect to the internet, collect analytics, or transmit any information whatsoever. All configuration is stored on your Mac. See the source code on GitHub to verify for yourself.

DESIGNED FOR MODERN macOS
Native SwiftUI throughout, built for macOS 13 Ventura and later. Automatically adopts light and dark appearance, respects Reduce Motion, and works seamlessly with notched MacBooks.

Made with love by the open source community. Contributions and translations welcome on GitHub.
